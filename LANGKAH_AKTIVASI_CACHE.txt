TROUBLESHOOTING CEPAT
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ Error: "Redis connection refused"
✅ Solusi:
   redis-cli ping
   Jika error, jalankan: redis-server

❌ Error: "ModuleNotFoundError: redis"
✅ Solusi:
   pip install redis==5.0.1

❌ Error: "Port 8000 already in use"
✅ Solusi:
   netstat -ano | findstr :8000
   taskkill /PID [PID] /F

❌ Cache hit rate rendah
✅ Solusi:
   - Pastikan Redis connected
   - Request endpoint yang sama berkali-kali
   - Check: curl http://localhost:8000/api/cache/stats
